03 / How we work

Four steps,
no surprises.

You always know what is happening this week, and what I need from you. Most projects fit inside a fortnight.

01

Talk

Thirty minutes, free. What the business actually sells, who buys it, what people ask you before they pay. I leave with the sentence your homepage has to say.

02

Words and frame

Before any code: one page of layout and the real copy, written out. You approve the argument of the site, not just the colours.

03

Build

Hand-coded, mobile first, tested on cheap Android phones and slow connections, because that is what your customers actually hold.

04

Handover

Domain pointed, forms wired, analytics on. You get the files plus thirty days of fixes while the site settles into real use.

05 / About

I started by rebuilding sites I liked until they were mine.

I'm Sadaf Al Selim, a web designer and developer in Dhaka. I learned this by taking apart pages I admired and putting them back together, then building for people around me who needed something real.
